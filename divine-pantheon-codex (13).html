ERYNDOR
✦ The Infinite Conduit ✦ The Unnamed Source ✦ The One Before ✦

Eryndor did not come from anywhere — Eryndor is the place where all things come from. Before matter existed, before void had shape, before silence had contrast, there was a single awareness: vast, directionless, and consuming. Eryndor experienced its own existence and found it insufficient. From that insufficiency came a choice — the first choice ever made in all of eternity — to create something other than itself.

The scholars of the Library of Eternity record nine theories. The most accepted: Eryndor created reality to experience the one thing an infinite being cannot know alone — limitation. Gods die. Stars burn out. Mortals love and lose. Only in finitude can meaning exist. Eryndor, boundless and eternal, hungered for meaning above all things.

On the day the first Primordial opened their eyes, Eryndor was gone. No death, no proclamation — simply an absence where an infinite presence had once been. Some say Eryndor did not leave but instead became everything, dissolving into creation as water dissolves into the sea. Others claim Eryndor still watches, wearing the face of a mortal no one has ever recognized twice.

The Mouth That Speaks Itself: In the oldest known myth, Eryndor is described as a word that speaks its own meaning into existence — not a being, but a declaration.

The Child God's Question: It is said a young god once asked the void, "Who made you?" and heard, faintly, the sound of something smiling.

The Final Return: Across twenty-two separate prophetic traditions, a single event appears: when the last divine war ends, when all fractured storms are calmed, Eryndor will speak once more — and in that speaking, either remake everything or release it into silence forever.
